Clarifications

Pricing FAQ

Why do custom cakes cost more than bakery cakes?

Custom wedding cakes are labor-intensive works of art. The price reflects not just the premium organic ingredients, but the hours spent designing, baking, cooling, filling, structuring with dowels, and meticulously decorating your centerpiece.

Does the tasting box fee go toward my final cake?

Yes! If you choose to book your wedding cake with SweetArt within 14 days of your tasting consultation, the $65 tasting box fee is fully credited toward your final cake balance.

Are dummy tiers cheaper than real cake?

Surprisingly, no. While the styrofoam core costs less than ingredients, dummy tiers require exactly the same amount of time, labor, fondant, and decoration as a real cake to look seamless. We price them at 80% of the cost of a real tier.
